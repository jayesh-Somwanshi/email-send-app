Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Our application uses Google OAuth to access your Gmail account. We only request the minimum permission necessary to send emails on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send).

We do not store your emails, contacts, or any personal data on our servers. All processing happens in real-time to facilitate the sending of your message.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your Google account information is used strictly to authenticate you and authorize the application to send the specific emails you compose within the app. We do not use your data for advertising or any other purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

We do not maintain a permanent database of your personal information. Session tokens are stored temporarily in your browser as cookies and in our server memory to maintain your login status. These tokens are deleted when you log out or when the session expires.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google APIs to provide the core functionality of this app. Your use of this application is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address provided in the developer console.
